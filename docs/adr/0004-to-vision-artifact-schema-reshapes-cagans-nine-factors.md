# to-vision's schema reshapes Cagan's nine factors into seven founder-scale fields

Cagan's nine-factor checklist (Business Objectives and Constraints, Customer Problems, Critical Insights, Enabling Technologies, Industry Trends, Competitive Landscape, Go-To-Market Considerations, Own Capabilities, Organizational Impacts) is the only itemized vision structure the research found, but it's enterprise-PM-scale and has no dedicated field for the falsifiable future-state claim the sharpness test demands. We considered keeping all nine as separate fields (rejected — fights the one-page/press-release altitude the research converges on, and several factors read as dead weight pre-launch) and folding "future state" into Business Objectives and Constraints (rejected — buries the one thing every source treats as vision's defining content). Instead, `to-vision`'s artifact has seven fields: **Vision Statement** (new, one-sentence headline), **Customer & Problem** (Cagan's Customer Problems, with the named customer folded in), **Future State** (new, the falsifiable future-state claim), **Grounding Insight** (Cagan's Critical Insights), **Why Us / Why Now** (Cagan's Business Objectives and Constraints, renamed for founder legibility), **Additional Grounding** (optional freeform, covers whichever of Cagan's remaining six factors apply, labeled inline), and **Vision Pivot Trigger** (new, the falsifiability condition per Cagan's vision-pivot/discovery-pivot split). Only the first five are mandatory. Artifact metadata (title, date, approval marker, revision marker) is explicitly excluded — inherited generically from Artifact storage & cross-reference convention (#7) and Approval & completion gate pattern (#9).
