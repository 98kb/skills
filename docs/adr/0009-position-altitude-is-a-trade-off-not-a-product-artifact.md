# A position is about a trade-off the founder faces, never about a thing the founder built

The stance record is frozen at ideation time, before a vision exists, which left its altitude undefined: pitched too abstractly it decides nothing, pitched too concretely it is the vision with extra steps. An illustrative sample got this wrong in the concrete direction — its positions were all product decisions (what counts as a food, one button, no cloud, no login), each dated to a `/to-vision` session, duplicating vision and pitch fields with nothing to catch the two drifting apart.

The band is fixed by a single test, readable off the entry alone in both directions: **does the entry name a trade-off, and can it be stated without naming an artifact of this product?** An entry naming no trade-off is too high and decides nothing — Cooper's finding that life goals "rarely figure directly into the design of specific elements." An entry naming a product artifact is too low and belongs in the vision. The band shape is **conflict class + standing disposition + episode reference**. This is not invented: Baron & Spranca define a protected value as *resistance to a trade-off*, so the strongest component the components research found is already this shape, and the rule generalises it to the whole artifact. That the check is a reading of the entry rather than a judgement call is load-bearing — the boundary research bought a *computable* "no entry covers this" check, and an altitude rule needing human judgement would have spent it.

Two facts had been collapsed into one. "Frozen at ideation time" establishes that no **vision artifact** exists; it does not establish that no **product idea** exists, and the failed sample failed exactly there. The product idea is the **scoping device** for a sitting and never the **subject** of an entry — a search light, not subject matter. Without it the sitting has no stopping condition and no source for the prompt list that Bond, Carlson & Keeney show is mandatory, free generation recovering roughly half a person's own objectives. Domain nouns are therefore admissible; product artifacts are not.

Ordering follows: the stance record is authored **before** the vision, **non-gating**, with no vision available to read. `to-vision` never requires a stance record and the stance record never waits on one. This makes the one-direction rule structural — a position cannot restate a vision field when there is no vision field to restate — but ordering is deliberately **not** the enforcement mechanism, because the rule must be timeless rather than temporal. If the record turns out to accrete, entries will be written years later with vision, pitch, roadmap and milestones all in context, every one of them in the failed sample's position. A seed-only rule would expire the moment that question is answered, so the test binds every entry ever written and ordering is a helpful accident at ideation.

The teeth are a **bar, not a cap**: an entry citing no dated episode is inadmissible, rather than admitted at reduced defensibility. A graded cap was rejected on the boundary research's own grounds — an uncited absolute is precisely Tetlock's declared absolute that bends under rhetorical reframing, and marking it *weaker* is a disclaimer, already rejected there on FRE 105's Advisory Committee Note. When no episode is forthcoming, the interview first presses for the specific past incident (McClelland's behavioural-event interview operating normally; refusing the general self-description *is* the method), and on failure the material is diverted to the dated open-questions register as a question the record is silent on. A register entry is not a weak position and never becomes loadable.

Two corrections to the component set follow. **Means objectives are excluded** — the components research carried Keeney's fundamental/means split across without asking which side was admissible, and a means objective is a product decision wearing an objective's clothes ("one button" is a means objective). **Decision episodes are promoted from peer component to evidence layer**, cited by every other entry; the research half-saw this in calling them "the carrier for everything above" without drawing the consequence. Net: three position types over one evidence type, not four peers. Protected values pass untouched; guiding principles survive on the band's support, their evidential backing being one practitioner source with no instrument.

The cost is accepted explicitly and must not be read as free. The episode bar makes the artifact *revealed* preference by construction, which is the only mitigation this effort has found for its strongest counter-evidence — Fagerlin & Schneider's measurement of the future-directed-personal-record genre failing at scale, roughly a third of stated preferences changing within two years. It also makes the artifact materially smaller, and what it rejects at a first sitting is the vivid uncited absolute the founder most wants to say. The go/no-go therefore chooses between a **small, defensible** artifact and a large, unfalsifiable one, not between this artifact and a bigger version of it.

This ADR settles altitude and the content-fixing half of ordering. The working admissibility instrument is schema and interview mechanics, and is not settled here; who loads the record and when, and whether `to-vision` consults it automatically, remain Phase 2 composition questions.
