# The stance record accretes by queued sitting, and no agent ever writes the founder-said layer

The stance record had been designed throughout on an unexamined premise: that it is written once, at ideation. That premise was never argued for, and it was generating problems rather than constraining them. It is why the components research wrote off calibration, consistency and drift — "requires records kept at decision time, which is structurally unavailable to a skill that writes the record once" — and it is why the strongest counter-evidence on this effort, Fagerlin & Schneider's measurement of the future-directed-personal-record genre failing at scale, had no candidate mitigation at all. A correction clears the way: an earlier framing offered continuous capture as a third horn and ruled it out as contradicting the frozen-record finding, but that finding forbids **in-place revision**, not **accretion**. Append-only growth under dated entries is what the problem-oriented medical record does with progress notes, what FRCP 30(e) does with errata, and what a decision journal does by design.

The record **accretes by queued sitting**: flags accumulate between sittings, the human runs `/to-stance` again, and entries are written by the founder in that sitting. Automatic appending at each downstream decision is rejected because an agent appending a position *is* an agent writing the founder-said layer, which is forbidden outright below. A frozen seed is rejected because it forfeits component recovery and the only available mitigation for the genre evidence while buying nothing — and because [ADR 0009](./0009-position-altitude-is-a-trade-off-not-a-product-artifact.md) already presupposes more than one sitting, both in routing inadmissible material to "a populated inbox at the first sitting" and in making its trade-off test deliberately **timeless** so that it binds an entry written in year three. Seed and accrual sittings write the **same component schema**; only the agenda differs, the seed being prompt-list-driven because objectives are not elicitable without one, the accrual being register-driven. A seed-specific schema was rejected as reintroducing the altitude problem at the boundary between the two.

**No agent may ever write into the founder-said layer**, on any trigger. An agent may append to the dated open-questions register, and it may write a derived entry marked as extrapolation — but **a derived entry gets no index line**. This is the point where two findings that were deliberately researched in ignorance of each other collide: the boundary research models extrapolation as a derived entry rather than a warning label, while the loadable-context research establishes that the index line is the *entire* discoverability surface and that lookup is lexical rather than ranked. An indexed derived entry is retrieved by exactly the mechanism that retrieves a founder-said position, and a weaker marker inside the entry does not reach a reader who has already decided to use it on the strength of the index match — the boundary research's own warning that vagueness "exacerbates [interpreters'] natural tendency to read documents in light of their own preferences" bites hardest here. Derived entries are therefore retrievable only on explicit request. If that leaves them near-useless, that is a finding about the derived-entry device, not a cost of this decision, and completing the reconciliation belongs to the mechanism-details ticket.

A register entry, when converted at a later sitting, **supplies the dated episode** ADR 0009's admissibility bar demands. It is written at decision time and records the decision that was made together with the question the record was silent on, which is contemporaneous capture and is a decision episode by that ADR's own definition. The register is therefore an **episode capture channel**, not merely the next sitting's agenda — a stronger claim than ADR 0009 made, and one that inverts the artifact's worst property: the seed sitting is episode-poor, and every later sitting arrives with episodes already banked, so admissibility improves rather than decays. The consequence lands on the open question of whether the register and an agent-flagged backlog are one mechanism or two: that split can no longer be made on workflow-convenience grounds, because one of the two halves is load-bearing record and cannot carry mutable workflow state.

Feasibility is established by a **stored next-sitting date**, checked passively on invocation in the shape [ADR 0007](./0007-to-milestone-cadence-target-check-in-passive-surfacing.md) already uses for Target Check-ins, with the computable "no entry covers this" flag as enrichment rather than mechanism. The ordering is deliberate and reverses the ticket's own proposal. The no-match flag fires only where someone consulted the record, likely the minority of decisions, and it is a lexical judgement over a routing table rather than a computation — the ADR 0007 precedent is a date comparison on a stored field, and the analogy is weaker than it looks. The date fires regardless of whether anyone opened the record; the flag makes a sitting *productive* when it happens rather than making it happen. Only the feasibility floor is settled here. The trigger's design — who checks, when, and how the two devices compose — is Phase 2 composition, alongside the already-deferred question of who loads the record.

Accretion **partially recovers two of the three components** named as decision-informing but not interview-reachable, and the recovery must not be overstated. Theory-in-use is the strongest: the espoused/in-use gap is precisely a stated position measured against decisions actually taken, register entries are behaviour recorded at decision time rather than self-report reconstructed later, and a sitting that opens with the register as its agenda reproduces Argyris & Schön's requirement that the observer confront the subject with the gap — but it samples only decisions where someone consulted the record, so the behavioural sample is biased rather than complete. Calibration and drift must be split: **consistency over time is recovered, true calibration is not**, since nothing here scores anything against outcomes. Cooper's goals are **not** recovered, his method requiring an observer inferring from observation, and the derived-entry route to them is closed by the no-index-line rule above. Both recoveries are a **derived view computed at read time over the series** — not a fifth elicited component and not a stored derived entry, so they never collide with that rule. Nothing new is elicited; the view is a way of reading what is already there.

Against Fagerlin & Schneider, accretion is a **partial** mitigation and the go/no-go must be told which part. Preference instability — roughly a third of stated preferences changing within two years — is genuinely mitigated, because a dated append-only series converts a stated future-directed preference into a time series of what the subject actually did, which is a different epistemic object. Their first condition, that people must have the record at all, is **not** mitigated: the stored date makes the reminder cheap, it does not make the sitting happen, and the failure is relocated from "nobody noticed" to "nobody showed up" rather than removed. Interpretive vagueness is untouched. Accretion therefore does not tip the go/no-go; it narrows it. What the decision session inherits is a sharper question than the one it held — *is a record that is thin at ideation and thickens only if the founder comes back worth building at all?*

Two things are deliberately left standing. The artifact category is **not** reopened: the decision journal, never scored as a genre because it was borrowed only as a donor for its one-named-subject property, becomes a **second donor** contributing the dated append-only lifecycle alongside IBIS contributing question-indexed positions, but it displaces nothing, because accretion changes neither the object nor the question-indexing that [ADR 0008](./0008-stance-record-replaces-persona-as-the-artifact-category.md) was decided on. And vocabulary stays unpinned per this map's standing note — no glossary terms are added here, the naming of components being the map's own destination.
