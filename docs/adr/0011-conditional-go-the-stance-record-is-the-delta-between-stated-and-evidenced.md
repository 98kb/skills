# Conditional go: the stance record's product is the delta between stated disposition and evidenced conduct

Phase 1 was gated on a real go/no-go — the research was to decide whether `to-stance` gets built at all, and a gate that can only open is not a gate. The evidence arrived split. Against: Fagerlin & Schneider measured the nearest-neighbour genre failing at scale, five conditions for a working future-directed personal record of which "not one condition has been achieved or, we think, can be"; the components research returned four decision-informing components drawn from four unrelated fields with **none** validated on a person recording their own stance for downstream reuse; and the prototype's seed cut of a real founder document yielded **zero** admissible positions, the entire record coming out as register. For: two research passes kept ignorant of each other converged from disjoint literatures on the same structure — an index of discrete positions each keyed to the question it was elicited about; the boundary mechanism survived being written and refused a misuse twice; and the five-donor assembly cohered with one identified seam.

**Decision: go, conditionally — and the artifact is not the one this effort set out to build.** Not *a record of a founder's stance*. It is **a record of trade-offs this founder has actually faced, pairing the standing disposition he states with the conduct that supports it, with the delta between the two visible and computable.** The name settled in ADR 0008 survives untouched; what changes is what the document is, not what it is called.

## The finding that carries the go, and it was filed as a cost

The prototype recorded that a founder states *"no co-founder, no investor, no employee — any of the three,"* while the episode that closes the rule reaches **only equity** — and that "the entry that survives is narrower than the thing he said, and the difference is silently discarded." That was written as the join's cost. It is the artifact's entire product. The gap between the rule a person asserts and the rule his conduct supports is Argyris & Schön's espoused-theory/theory-in-use gap, which the components research named as the most valuable component of all *and* placed permanently out of reach of any interview, because the gap is by construction invisible to the person espousing. The episode bar surfaces it mechanically, per entry, without anyone having to observe the founder for years. Nothing else in a sixteen-category corpus does this — not a persona, not a principles register, not an advance directive.

So the delta is not discarded. **A position records the standing disposition verbatim, the evidenced scope its cited episodes actually support, and the residue — the part of the stated disposition no episode reaches — marked as stated and unevidenced.** The residue is simultaneously reopened as an entry in the open-questions register, so the next sitting presses for the incident that would close it. This makes the register a *product of* position-writing rather than a leftover bin, and it makes the record's own gaps drive its growth.

**The residue is recorded but never indexed.** The stance index routes on the evidenced scope only. This is the mechanism, and it is load-bearing in two directions. The prototype wrote the never-list both ways and found the unusable version — *what he said he would never trade* — is the shorter, more quotable one that sounds more like him, so the pull toward it is constant and comes from the founder himself. And Fagerlin & Schneider's sharpest mechanism is that vagueness is not neutral: "the imprecision of living wills not only stymies interpreters, it exacerbates their natural tendency to read documents in light of their own preferences." An unevidenced absolute with no routing line cannot be retrieved, so it cannot be projected onto. The prototype had already established the general fact in a different context, as a defect: an entry with no index line is unreachable. Here it is the quarantine.

This is also the answer to the requirement that the artifact carry a designed-in distinction between what the founder said and what a reader might extrapolate, "not a warning label." **The stated/evidenced delta *is* that distinction** — structural, per-entry, and computable — which is what the boundary research demanded after rejecting disclaimers on FRE 105's own Advisory Committee Note.

## Fagerlin & Schneider read as a specification rather than an objection

Their five conditions were treated as the strongest counter-evidence Phase 1 produced. Under the design as decided, three of the five are answered and one is refused outright. *People must have the record* — **unmitigated**, and named below as this artifact's largest risk. *They must decide what they would want in a future situation* — **refused by construction**: the dated-episode bar admits retrospective conduct only, so the record never asks the founder to predict anything, which is the condition their whole critique turns on ("work out exactly what rule they want, imagine all the circumstances in which it might apply, find language to specify all those but only those circumstances. Each task is ultimately impossible"). *They must state it accurately and lucidly* — **substantially met**: the question key makes application computable, and the residue quarantine removes the vague part from the retrieval path. *It must be available to decision-makers* — **trivially met**, an always-loaded index in the repo. *Readers must grasp and heed it* — **"heed" does not transfer**, because the never-an-authority constraint means the record never directs.

This is not a rebuttal. Their paper is the best-measured evidence this effort found, and it is used here as a design brief. What survives is preference instability, mitigated by the dated series and not by anything else, and their first condition, which is not mitigated at all.

## The component list

Position types are **dissolved**. This amends ADR 0009, which concluded "three position types over one evidence type, not four peers" and let guiding principles survive on the band's support. The prototype's cut shows why that cannot hold: protected values and guiding principles arrived in identical shape — standing rules with no occasion attached — and the bar treated them identically. A distinction the schema never acts on is not a component. Worse, three of the cut's five candidates were guiding principles, which is the component with the weakest evidential backing of the four (one practitioner source, no instrument) carrying the most weight — and it is also the drift the category research warned about by name, calling the principles/credo register "the most dangerous near-miss, because the shape fits and the standing is exactly wrong." Dissolving the types closes that structurally: with no guiding-principles component there is nothing for a credo register to drift into. **Baron & Spranca's instrument and Keeney's prompt list are retained as interview instruments, not as entry categories**, and ADR 0009's exclusion of means objectives stands. Differentiation is carried by **conflict class**, which ADR 0009's band shape already specified.

The record therefore holds two entry kinds, one working layer and one quarantined layer. An **episode** is a dated occasion — what was at stake, what he did, the reason he gave — and is the evidence layer every position cites. A **position** is a trade-off he faces, backed by at least one dated episode. An **open-questions register entry** is a trade-off named with no episode closing it. A **derived entry** is the recorder's inference, carries no index line, and remains inert pending the mechanism ticket.

A position carries: the question it was elicited about; conflict class; standing disposition, verbatim; evidenced scope; residue; the basis he gave; a defensibility cap; and citations to episodes. Two changes from the structure assembled before the prototype. **"Position, verbatim then restated" splits into disposition, evidenced scope and residue** — the schema change that carries the go. And **"situation it was formed in" is dropped**: APA Record Keeping Guideline 8's field is subsumed, because under the bar no position exists without a dated episode and the episode *is* the situation. A field always derivable from a mandatory relation is not a field.

**The index line is a component and is authored outside the entry.** The context research established that a position with no index line will never be retrieved, making index wording the entire discoverability surface; the prototype then found that adding one position can silently narrow another's line, so lines must be revisable while entries are frozen. Authoring an index line is therefore a separate act with its own provenance, covering the evidenced scope only.

## The bar, ratified strictly, with the distinction that was actually missing

ADR 0009's teeth read "an entry citing no dated episode is inadmissible," and the prototype found that one word decides the output: under the bar as written the seed cut yields zero entries, under "an episode, dated where available" it yields one. **Ratified as written.** The date is the entire basis of ADR 0010's mitigation of preference instability — a record without dates is not a time series, it is stated preference with a timestamp missing — and loosening buys one entry at the cost of the only mitigation the artifact has. That is padding, which the components research warned against by name in telling this effort to weigh the thinness rather than pad it.

What does change is a **date-provenance field on every episode**, contemporaneous or recalled. The prototype found the register is two mechanisms wearing one name: a seed-opened entry has its date recalled months later, an agent-appended entry is written at the decision, and both clear a bar that checks for *a date* and not for how the date was known. They are not equal evidence, and ADR 0010's mitigation holds for the contemporaneous half only.

## Empty at the seed, by design

The seed sitting produces no positions. The prototype established this is structural rather than an accident of one file: dispositions and episodes are elicited by opposite questions, and a founder asked what he thinks returns standing rules, never occasions. **The register is the seed sitting's deliverable, and this is stated as the design rather than conceded as a shortfall.** What `to-stance` hands a founder at t0 is not his stance but the list of convictions he states and has not tested, plus a primed episode-capture channel.

That reframe is the only mitigation available for Fagerlin & Schneider's unmitigated first condition. A stored next-sitting date makes the reminder cheap, not the sitting likely; what makes a founder come back is the seed sitting having told him something he wanted to know. The alternative — lowering the bar so the seed record is not empty — is the padding path and contradicts the paragraph above.

## Lifecycle, reach, and checks

**Frozen and superseded by dated append, ratified — but on a stronger ground than the boundary research gave.** That pass excluded design personas, the one discipline that revises freely, on the grounds that their object is a design target rather than a record of a person, and named that as where its recommendation was most contestable. The definitional claim is arguable; the operational one is not. Revisable entries cannot be a time series, and the time series is the only mitigation this artifact has. Design personas revise freely precisely *because* they carry no falsifiability claim to protect — which is Chapman & Milham's whole critique of them. This re-affirms the known contradiction with the approval-and-completion-gate pattern decided on the sibling milestone map; that remains flagged, not resolved here.

**Theory-in-use moves in reach.** ADR 0010 recovered it as a read-time derived view requiring accumulation. The stated/evidenced delta surfaces it **per entry, at the first sitting** — the components research's "most valuable of all if you could get it," obtained at t0. **Cooper's goals and true calibration stay out**, and calibration's reason is worth recording for consistency: it requires *outcomes*, and admitting outcomes would re-import the prospective frame the bar refuses. It stays out by the same rule that makes the artifact defensible.

**No third-party accuracy test exists** — the boundary research's finding, ratified, and it moves the eval seam, which this map has ruled out of scope. Three checks survive, and the third is new. A deterministic provenance-and-scope audit. A founder-resolved prediction test. And **residue coverage**: an entry whose residue is empty means a stated rule fully closed by conduct, which on the prototype's evidence should be rare, so a record where most entries have empty residue is a record whose interview is not pressing. It is record-level, gradeable by anyone, needs no founder, and measures the one thing the artifact exists to do.

## The donor set, and where the assembly ends

**IBIS is the genre donor** — question-indexed positions, and never-an-authority enforced structurally by having no stopping rule rather than by disclaimer. POMR, ISAD(G)/DACS, PROV-O and the decision journal are mechanism donors. The prototype confirmed the five compose without fighting, with one seam between POMR's live problem list and ISAD(G)'s closed record, which is a mechanism ticket's to settle. **The stated/evidenced delta comes from no donor.** It is this artifact's own contribution, and it is where "stance record" stops being an assembly and becomes a genre — the cost the category research said would have to be paid deliberately rather than discovered in Phase 2. This is paying it.

One transposition is flagged rather than inherited. Every mechanism borrowed from the boundary research was validated on a **human** reader, and this record's reader is usually an agent. The prototype's scope cap held twice against an agent reader, which is one positive data point. But **"no disclaimers anywhere" does not survive the transposition and must not be carried into Phase 2 as settled**: it rests on FRE 105's Advisory Committee Note, a finding about human juries failing to unhear inadmissible evidence, and an agent reader has no such psychology.

## The cost, and the condition under which this go was wrong

The yield of a real sitting has never been measured and is not measured before this decision. Running one was considered and rejected: the only founder available is the person who designed the bar, so a sitting with him buys the appearance of a yield measurement rather than one. The Phase 1 question is whether the mechanism does something nothing else does, and the prototype answers that; yield is a Phase 2 risk.

**The go is therefore conditional, stated falsifiably so Phase 2 inherits a checkpoint rather than a mandate. If the first seed sitting with a founder who does not know the bar yields zero admissible positions *and* a register he does not return for, the no-go stands and Phase 2 stops.** That is the deferred measurement, and this is where it lands.
