# A routing line's extent is fixed by evidenced scope; revision is a wording act, and its provenance lives on the index

[ADR 0011](0011-conditional-go-the-stance-record-is-the-delta-between-stated-and-evidenced.md) froze positions and left the index over them revisable, per ISAD(G)/DACS. The [`jian-yang.md` re-cut](https://github.com/98kb/skills/tree/prototype/stance-recut/prototypes/stance-recut) then exercised that split under growth, and reported it as follows: at the accrual sitting P101's routing line had to be narrowed because P102 arrived at the same sitting and the broad wording swallowed P102's queries; nothing illegal happened; and yet the same frozen entry, unedited, now returns for a different set of questions than it did before, so a reader on the day before and the day after gets different behaviour out of an identical record.

**Neither half of that account survives contact with the prototype's own files, and the two corrections point the same way.** The narrowing happened *before publication* — the prototype's index says so, and revision 1 held no routing lines at all — so no reader ever retrieved under the broad wording and the day-before/day-after reader does not exist. And the broad draft was not legal: it was already in breach when it was written, before P102 existed. What the prototype found is not a collision between two lines. It is a single line that was wrong on its own terms.

## The extent is fixed by the record; only the wording is authored

ADR 0011 bounds a routing line from above: a line covers a position's **evidenced scope only**, because a line reaching past it routes to the **residue**, and an unevidenced absolute that cannot be retrieved cannot be projected onto. That ceiling is the never-an-authority quarantine, and it was written for the residue rather than for this.

This ADR adds a **floor**: a line may not be narrower than evidenced scope either. That is a **new rule, adopted rather than derived**, and the honest case against it should be recorded alongside it. Against: the computable no-match is defined per position — ADR 0012's "the absence of an index line *is* the computable no-match" — so at the granularity the design actually specifies, a narrow line still exists and the no-match does not lie. And the design already treats deliberate unroutability as correct twice, in the residue quarantine and in ADR 0010's derived entries.

The floor is adopted anyway, because those two precedents are **recorded refusals** and a narrow line is a silent one. The residue is quarantined *and written down and reopened in the register*; a derived entry is unroutable *and says so*. A line narrower than its evidenced scope leaves evidenced material unreachable while the record shows nothing — the reader gets silence where the record holds an answer, and no artifact anywhere says a choice was made. Recall costs the design accepts elsewhere are stated; this one would not be.

Ceiling and floor land in the same place: **a routing line is co-extensive with its position's evidenced scope, so its extent is fixed by the record and only its wording is authored.**

Read back against the prototype, this reframes what happened at T1. P101's evidenced scope is "equity and debt from outside parties," and its own defensibility cap adds "does not cover paid labour with no continuing claim." The rejected draft — *"outside help, money, or people — investors, co-founders, contractors, hires"* — reaches past both. It was in breach of the ceiling when written, before P102 arrived. The published line reaches equity and debt and nothing else, and the prototype's own note describes the repair as narrowing "to the instruments P101's episode actually reaches" — co-extensive with evidenced scope, arrived at by hand and without the invariant being consulted.

## Collision splits in two, and only one half is answered here

An **over-broad line** is a breach of the ceiling, and it is caught by checking a line against **its own position** rather than against the other lines. That check needs no other line and no knowledge of what has been written since, which is what disposes of the ticket's worry about a two-year-old line colliding with a position added today: the check never depended on the other lines, so there was never a moment to miss. This is the prototype's case, and it is the half this ADR answers.

**The check is a lexical judgement, not a computation, and it is not a falsifiability check.** Comparing a prose routing line's reach against a prose evidenced-scope statement is exactly what ADR 0010 called "a lexical judgement over a routing table rather than a computation" when it declined to rest the no-match flag on one. Nothing here is stronger. What the invariant buys is a **fixed referent** — the judgement is made against one frozen position instead of against an open-ended set of other lines — which makes it cheap, repeatable and non-decaying, and that is the whole of the improvement. It is a sharper prompt, not a fourth check alongside ADR 0011's residue coverage.

The other half — **two legal lines over genuinely adjacent evidenced scopes** — is **not settled here, and the design does not currently specify what happens.** Retrieval is lexical and unranked, so there is no defined behaviour for two matches, and the only observation on record runs the wrong way: the prototype found that competing lines do not both return, "the broader line wins by covering more of them." Adjacency is not an edge case either, since ADR 0011's residue-to-register-to-next-position loop manufactures abutting scopes by construction, and a superseded position and its successor are the paradigm case. Whether a superseded position keeps its routing line at all is unowned by the same gap. This goes to a fresh ticket rather than being waved through here.

## An agent may author and revise routing lines; `described-by` is the only marker

[ADR 0010](0010-stance-record-accretes-by-queued-sitting.md) enumerates what an agent may write — register appends and derived entries — and the index is not on that list. This ADR **extends that enumeration explicitly rather than reading the index in under it**: an agent may author and revise routing lines. A line is authored at the sitting that publishes its position; *when* the per-line check runs against existing lines thereafter is a workflow question and belongs to the skill, not here.

The bound on that power is the section above, and it is narrower than it first looks. A routing line **does** introduce authored material: P101's published line enumerates "an angel, a VC, a convertible note, a co-founder taking stock," none of which appears in the question key, the evidenced scope or the conflict class. It cannot be otherwise — a line states when to consult a position *in the vocabulary a downstream dilemma would arrive in*, which is by construction vocabulary the record does not contain. Supplying that vocabulary is the line's job. What a reviser may not introduce is **reach**: the words may change, what they are permitted to reach may not. That is a real bound and it is the only one, and since retrieval is lexical the gap between an intended extent and the extent the words actually get is where the residual risk sits.

Two further limits are worth naming. Evidenced scope is itself a recorder's derivation from the cited episodes, not founder-said verbatim, so "extent fixed by the record" means fixed by a prior recorded interpretation rather than by the founder's own words. And the log preserves superseded wording, so a narrowing is reversible by inspection.

[ADR 0012](0012-to-stance-mechanisms-computed-over-declared-and-approval-is-authorship.md) left one clause of the [approval & completion gate pattern (#9)](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/9) alive for this ticket: an approval marker clearing back to "drafted" on in-place revision, the index being its only revisable subject. **The answer is the same — no marker; `described-by` is the marker — but not for ADR 0012's reason, and the difference should not be papered over.** ADR 0012 collapsed the marker because "no agent ever writes the founder-said layer, so for a position there is no drafted state to leave, and the marker would record a transition that cannot occur." This ADR makes an agent an author of the index, so on the index that transition **can** occur, for the first time in the artifact. The premise ADR 0012 relied on is inverted here, and the conclusion is re-reached on a weaker ground: `described-by` records who authored, the reach is bounded by evidenced scope either way, and a blessing marker on description would be the first thing in the design a human is asked to sign that nothing may gate on. **The residual is stated rather than dismissed: an agent may publish and later reword the artifact's entire discoverability surface, and what distinguishes that from founder-authored routing is an authorship field, not an approval.**

## Nothing lands in the position file — on the quarantine, not the freeze

The obvious objection to noting a routing revision inside the position is that a mutable field on a frozen record breaks the freeze. **That objection is wrong, and it matters that it is.** The rule is *frozen ≠ static: superseded only by dated append*, and a dated append reading "routing revised 2027-02-15" breaks nothing.

The real objection is the **ISAD(G)/DACS quarantine**: description facts recorded inside the record contaminate the layer the split exists to keep clean, and the next thing offered for that slot will be less inert. Refused on the quarantine, the answer survives even if the freeze rule is later loosened.

## What is owed to a reader is reconstruction, not notification

ADR 0012 found that **nothing in this design persists a retrieval**, which is why it refused the claim that a deliberate lookup is visible and attributable. The design therefore cannot identify who retrieved under superseded wording, and a notice addressed to an audience it cannot name would be a disclaimer in the sense ADR 0011 refused — a device that asks the reader to hold something in mind rather than changing what the reader can reach. That refusal rests on a design-capability argument, not on the human-reader-to-agent-reader transposition failure ADR 0012 catalogued; the two are different objections and this ADR does not add to that tally.

What is owed is that superseded behaviour be **reconstructible from the record**: a dated revision log carrying `described-by`, the superseded wording, the reason, and the revision's **kind** — *wording-only*, where the extent is unchanged, versus *corrective*, where the extent breached a bound and the record would have misled anyone who did retrieve. In principle `kind` is derivable, since *corrective* means the superseded wording failed the per-line check; but that check is a judgement rather than a computation, so what the log stores is a recorded judgement. That is the most a design with no retrieval persistence can honestly offer.

## Description control splits by growth curve

The prototype's description-control block is adopted, split across tiers by how each part grows. Constant-size fields stay in the always-loaded tier: `described-by`, description date, revision number, and **`covers`** — the span of positions the current description accounts for, which is retained rather than dropped because the description date alone tells a reader how old the description is and not whether it has fallen behind the record. The dated revision log goes off-tier, retrieved on request, because it accretes one entry per revision forever against ADR 0012's byte tripwire, and only someone auditing a specific change needs the superseded wording.

This is a precedent for [the register's relation to the always-loaded tier (#112)](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/112), **not a pre-emption of it** — that ticket owns the tripwire's trip action and the general question of what sheds.

## ADR 0012's computed-over-declared shape does not extend to the index

A recorded `kind` is a declared field, which reads as a violation of the shape ADR 0012 called forced. It is not, and the reason is worth stating. **Computed-over-declared was forced by the freeze**: a declared field on a frozen record either mutates and breaks it or goes stale. The index is description — not frozen, revisable by design, carrying its own revision number — so the premise does not hold and the shape does not extend. This is recorded explicitly because this map already caught itself nearly reading [ADR 0003](0003-eval-grading-is-deterministic-plus-llm-judge-split.md)'s eval bar as repo-wide against its own first sentence.

ADR 0012's *second* objection to declared fields is freeze-independent and does apply: being prose, a declared field is what the boundary research says gets re-read in light of the reader's own preferences. It is accepted as a cost here, because `kind` sits off-tier in the revision log where nothing routes on it, and because the superseded wording sits beside it for a reader who distrusts the label.
