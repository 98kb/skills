# A brand claim is admitted by a named particular, never by a judgement, and which particular is demanded depends on the claim class

[Map: to-brand-identity (#136)](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/136) was chartered against a stated risk: brand identity is the most vibes-prone field this pipeline has touched, *"we value quality, innovation, and putting customers first"* is a real output of a real brand workshop, and a skill that produces it is a workshop-generator. Every other artifact in this repo carries a discipline that makes an entry **inadmissible** — a Riskiest Assumption needs claim/threshold/test/timebox, a Position needs a dated episode, a Move needs a signal and a threshold — and `to-brand-identity` had none.

[#139](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/139) established that a bar exists, published and named, clearing the map's two-independent-practitioners source requirement several times over. This ADR takes the decision [#140](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/140) owns: the exact formulation, its scope, its timing, and what happens to a claim that fails.

## The instrument is selected by claim class, because a single bar produces false rejections either way

The corpus contradicts itself under a universal bar, and the contradiction is not a muddle in the sources — it is a boundary between two objects that happen to share a word.

*"We hold safety above schedule"* **fails** the reversal test, because nobody advocates the reverse, and **passes** Collins & Porras's gate outright, because it costs you shipped revenue and you would hold it anyway. Trout's *Differentiate or Die* is right that quality and customer orientation are rarely differentiating ideas; Collins's workbook is right that a value every competitor also holds is admissible provided it is paid for. Both are right about their own object. Neumeier's onlyness sentence exists to force differentiation; Collins & Porras deny differentiation is the job at all — *"the role of core ideology is to guide and inspire, not to differentiate."* Two independent passes reached this from different directions: [#139](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/139) from the strategy literature and [#138](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/138) from the sources' own words, where it is named a **field boundary**.

So the artifact needs more than one bar, and which one applies is fixed by the **claim class**:

- **Positioning claim** — reversal, then cost.
- **Value** — cost only. Reversal is refused here by construction, since Collins's own criterion admits values every competitor shares.
- **Voice attribute** — a constructed test; see below.
- **Distinctive asset** — **exempt.** On Ehrenberg-Bass's evidence a colour, mark or mascot must be *recognisable*, not *arguable*, and running a reversal test on a colour is a category error. This is why the map's scope decision to reduce visual identity to a designer's brief matters structurally rather than merely practically: the brief's contents are exempt **by design**.

**The cost of this split, stated rather than smuggled: claim class is load-bearing, and misfiling is the escape hatch.** A positioning claim that fails reversal (*"we're easy to use"*) could be refiled as a value, where reversal does not apply, and then plausibly clear the cost test. The leak is closed by two rules. **Class is fixed before elicitation, not after** — the interview asks for values in a values pass and positioning in a positioning pass, so a claim is never in flight looking for a home. And **a failing claim is never moved sideways into a class with a different bar**; the routing rule below only ever moves it *down*, into a section that makes no differentiating pretence.

## Admissibility is computed from a named particular, because a judgement is the thing that drifts

The bar's formulation, in the form the agent applies it:

- **Reversal** passes iff the founder names a **named alternative** — a real firm running the opposite. Not a hypothetical, not a category.
- **Cost** passes iff the founder names a **forfeit** — a specific thing this claim costs them: a segment refused, a feature declined, a deal walked away from. For a value, the forfeit is a **dated episode** in which holding it cost something.

The alternative designs are worse in ways that are easy to state. If **the agent judges** the claim, the bar is an LLM's opinion about whether a sentence is differentiated, which is exactly the vibes the map was chartered against and is ungradable by an eval. If **the founder self-certifies** — the agent asks, the founder says yes — the skill is a form, which is the other thing the map was chartered against. Collins's seven-question gate, the only fully-specified portable instrument in the entire corpus, is self-certification of this second kind: all seven are counterfactuals about the founder's own future conduct (*would* you hold it if penalised, *would* you change jobs, *would* you keep it if rich).

Making the verdict a **presence check over a named particular** puts this artifact in line with every other one in the repo. The agent checks that a proper noun or a dated particular is present and specific; it does not check that the claim is good. That is the same move as *a Position is inadmissible without a dated episode*, and it was reached here independently, from Porter, Popper and Collins rather than from archival practice.

**The specificity rule is mechanical, so that "presence check" is not itself a judgement.** The named particular must be a **proper noun or a dated particular** — a named firm, a named segment, a named feature, a named deal. *"Our competitors"*, *"some customers"*, *"certain enterprise features"* fail on their face. Dunford's bound applies to the firm: it counts only if the founder has **lost to it or been compared against it**, because *"they are not a true alternative if they don't show up on a shortlist."*

**The founder is never asked to invent the opposite.** This is a sourced correction rather than a preference, and two unrelated literatures produced it. [#138](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/138) found Kelly's repertory grid names the defect — **"bent constructs"** — and documents the field's preference for the *difference method* over the *opposites method*; [#139](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/139) reached the same correction from Martin and Dunford, and noted that the map's original phrasing (*"could a credible competitor sincerely claim the opposite"*) asks about a rival's marketing copy, where Martin's (*"is the opposite stupid on its face"*) and Cohen's (*"could anyone construct a rational strategy with that negative"*) ask about the viability of an opposing position. Naming a real firm is a fact-shaped question. Imagining a rival's copy is not.

### Two exceptions, carried deliberately, because the bar over-rejects without them

**Cohen's 10x carve-out.** A generic claim is admissible when it is the single biggest differentiator and the founder is *"truly 10x better"* at it — *"Fastest,"* with benchmarks. Its named particular is **the benchmark itself**, a number and its source, and Cohen's own cost stays attached: it may be claimed **once**, not sprinkled across the artifact.

**Dunford's combination allowance.** Uniqueness may live in the bundle rather than in any single attribute. The named alternative is therefore demanded at the **bundle** level — a firm running a *different bundle* — and not once per attribute, or legitimate combination positioning is rejected attribute by attribute.

### Voice is a construction, and is recorded as one

[#139](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/139) reports a genuine empty result: **no published, named admissibility test for a voice attribute exists.** NN/g's four two-poled dimensions are the closest reusable device, and Mailchimp's *"we're weird but not inappropriate,"* *"we prefer winking to shouting"* is the test performed but published as one company's style guide, not as method.

The obvious construction — make the founder name the pole they are rejecting — is founder-invented, and therefore the bent-construct defect again. So the voice test demands two particulars external to the founder's imagination: **a named brand whose voice sits at the opposite pole**, and **one specimen sentence this brand would refuse to publish.** The refused sentence is Popper's prohibition criterion in the voice register — *every genuine theory is prohibitive* — and it is what Mailchimp's contrast pairs actually encode.

**This rests on NN/g plus the general instrument and on no voice-specific authority. It does not clear the map's two-practitioners bar and is not claimed to.** It is adopted as a construction; if it fails in evals, nothing else in this ADR moves with it.

## The bar runs live and unannounced, with one closing read

Martin's repair instruction — *"drive the choice to a greater and greater level of specificity until such time as you can say with confidence that the opposite is not stupid on its face"* — is a conversational loop. In a batch pass it is dead on arrival: the founder gets a rejection list at the point they can no longer repair it in context.

Against that, [#138](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/138) found the shared cause of all four elicitation failure modes is that **the moment the founder believes the output is copy, they pre-filter for quotability.** A visibly announced bar teaches the founder the shape of a passing answer, and a gamed bar is worse than no bar, because it launders filler as evidenced.

Both are respected by asking for the named alternative and the forfeit as **ordinary interview questions inside the elicitation** — which is what Wheeler's founder interview, Kelly's triadic elicitation and Dunford's competitive-alternatives step all do anyway — and never framing them as a test the claim must pass. A **closing read** then runs once over the assembled set, for what a live loop structurally cannot see: duplicate claims, class drift, and Rumelt's audit, reading the claims back as a set and asking what a reader would conclude the company refuses to do.

**Pressing is bounded at one round, and the branch choice belongs to the founder.** A second press is where manufacturing starts. If the repaired attempt still yields no named particular, the agent does not decide *why* it failed — it asks: is this something you and everyone else already do, something you want to be true but are not yet, or something that is not there yet? Handing the classification to the founder is the same move as handing them the named particular, and it keeps the agent out of the one judgement that would make the routing arbitrary.

## A failing claim is routed, not deleted — in four directions

Rejection is the wrong output; the sources are unusually specific and they agree on three destinations sorted by *why* the claim failed, and this design adds a fourth for a case they do not cover.

1. **Too vague → sharpen.** Martin: drive to greater specificity. His worked case is a luxury hotel, where generic guest-centricity fails but a particular Four Seasons practice passes. The claim was not wrong; it was pitched too high. One press, per above.
2. **Genuinely held but universal → reclassify as table stakes.** Trout for product claims, Lencioni's *permission-to-play* for values. The claim survives, in a section that makes no differentiating pretence.
3. **Held in the future, not the past → labelled aspiration.** Collins is explicit and gives the consequence: a group that has never held innovation as a core value should put it in the Envisioned Future, because *"do not mix future aspirations into your true and authentic core values, as this will create justifiable cynicism and destroy the power of your core values."*
4. **Nothing there at all → stop writing and say so.** Neumeier: *"if you can't say why your brand is both different and compelling in a few words, don't fix your positioning statement…fix your company."* Dunford: *"positioning isn't a band-aid we slap on a weak product to prop it up in the market."* Cohen: dismiss it as trivial, obvious, mandatory. **This is the branch a brand skill is most tempted to suppress and it has the strongest independent backing of the three.** This ADR fixes only that the branch exists and that the founder's own classification selects it; what the skill leaves behind when it fires is [#147](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/147)'s decision.

### The uncosted claim, and why the quarantine invariant transposes but does not transfer

There is a fifth case the literature does not route, and it is the one that maps onto machinery this repo already has: **a claim that passes reversal, fails cost, and the founder insists on anyway.** That is [ADR 0011](0011-conditional-go-the-stance-record-is-the-delta-between-stated-and-evidenced.md)'s stated-versus-evidenced delta arriving in a new register — a *stated* position the company has not actually taken.

It is handled by the stance record's quarantine invariant: **recorded, marked stated-and-uncosted, and never retrievable as a brand claim.** A downstream reader or agent can never quote it; the founder's assertion is not silently binned; the next session has an agenda. That is `residue`'s shape exactly.

**It is nonetheless a different thing, and it gets a different name for one reason.** `residue` is frozen, and [ADR 0015](0015-two-legal-routing-lines-are-correct-and-a-position-is-never-superseded.md) rests that freeze on conduct not being undone by whatever the person decides afterwards. **A brand claim's forfeit can genuinely arrive later** — the founder walks the deal next quarter — so an **uncosted claim is closable**, by a later dated forfeit that promotes it to an ordinary admitted claim. Calling it `residue` would import a freeze the object cannot bear.

## Six-sevenths of the corpus's best instrument is discarded, and the reason is on the record

Collins & Porras's gate is the only fully-specified, reusable, published admissibility checklist found anywhere in the corpus: a per-candidate list, an all-must-pass rule, a named load-bearing question, a cap of three to five, a two-thirds quorum. Discarding most of it needs saying out loud.

**Question 1 is kept as the wording of the interview question** — *"would you want your organization to hold this core value, even if at some point in time it became a competitive disadvantage — even if in some instances the environment penalized the organization for living this core value?"* — because it is better phrased than anything we would write, and it is the question that elicits a forfeit.

**The admissibility condition is the dated episode, not the answer to that question.** The remaining six are counterfactuals, and a counterfactual is self-certification under a different grammar.

**Collins's routing is kept in full** — a genuinely-held value with no episode yet is not rejected, it becomes a labelled aspiration, which is his own instruction. So this departs from his *test* while keeping his *routing*.

Collins's 3–5 cap and two-thirds quorum are schema and belong to [#141](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/141), not here. And the whole port is a **transposition**: Collins wrote for an organisation's enduring values, not a founder's claims about a product. [#139](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/139) flagged that and this ADR carries the flag rather than resolving it.

## Consequences

- **The eval suite gets deterministic checks for free.** *Proper noun or dated particular* is mechanically gradable, which is what [ADR 0003](0003-eval-grading-is-deterministic-plus-llm-judge-split.md)'s deterministic half wants; the LLM-judge half is left with the conversation, not the verdict. Recorded on [#145](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/145).
- **The interview design is constrained before it is designed.** Live-and-unannounced, one press, founder-classifies. Recorded on [#142](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/142).
- **The artifact schema now has a fixed set of destinations to place**: admitted claims per class, a table-stakes section, a labelled-aspiration section, and quarantined uncosted claims. [#141](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/141) places them; this ADR does not.
- **The skill can end a session without producing a brand artifact.** That is the intended behaviour of branch 4, and it is the single strongest signal that this skill is not a form.
- **Voice is the weak leg and is known to be.** If the constructed test fails, positioning and values are untouched.
