# A brand identity accretes by claim-class sitting, and the freeze falls between the evidence and the claim

Three lifecycle questions were put together — one sitting or many, whether there is an approval marker at all, and what a re-entry does — and they resolve with one shape. **Every lifecycle fact this artifact needs is computed from what is already on disk, and the single thing that is declared, the freeze, falls between the evidence and the claim rather than between two artifacts.** [ADR 0012](0012-to-stance-mechanisms-computed-over-declared-and-approval-is-authorship.md) reached computed-over-declared under a *frozen* record, on the argument that a declared field on a frozen record is either mutable and breaks the freeze or immutable and goes stale. This record does the opposite thing — it grows — and the same shape is forced from the other side: a declared field on a growing record goes stale at the next sitting.

## Many sittings, and the determinant is mechanical rather than a matter of ambition

[#138](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/138) is unanimous. Every source in the corpus claiming to produce a finished identity is multi-session — Wheeler's five gated phases, Aaker's three, Collins's breakouts plus a Mars Group pass, Seidman's three ninety-minute interviews spaced three days to a week apart, ZMET's seven-to-ten days of homework before a two-hour sitting — and the only genuinely single-sitting instrument found, Knapp's three-hour brand sprint, bills itself as *"GV's Simple Recipe For Getting Started On Your Brand."* The two determinants it names are mechanical: **material the founder must go and find cannot be found in the room**, and **a question whose form depends on a prior answer cannot be asked in the same sitting as that answer.**

Both hold here *because of* [ADR 0017](0017-a-brand-claim-is-admitted-by-a-named-particular-and-the-instrument-is-selected-by-claim-class.md) rather than in general. The bar demands a **named alternative** and a **forfeit**, and a forfeit — a dated act, a segment refused, a deal walked — is precisely the kind of thing a founder leaves the room to check.

**Both stopping rules are adopted, at different scopes.** Saturation ends an elicitation *pass* — the construct literature's "until the participant can no longer think of meaningful distinctions" — and a clock ends the *sitting*, at the ninety-minute-to-two-hour mark Seidman, Zaltman and Wheeler independently land near. Where saturation has not arrived by the clock, the answer is **another sitting, not a longer one**; Seidman's reason is that past that point "a situation of diminishing returns sets in."

**Accretion is open-ended, and the sitting's unit is the claim-class pass.** No fixed series is adopted, against Seidman's three-interview shape and Wheeler's gated phases, because ADR 0017 already forbids a mixed pass and therefore already fixes the boundary — one class per sitting by default, ended by saturation. A fixed series would have to invent a job for a third sitting that ADR 0017 does not have. [ADR 0010](0010-stance-record-accretes-by-queued-sitting.md)'s refusal of a seed-specific schema is inherited unchanged: every sitting writes the same schema, and what varies is which class is in scope and what is on the queue, never what a sitting is permitted to write.

**The first sitting is expected to admit claims, and this is a real divergence from `to-stance`.** [ADR 0011](0011-conditional-go-the-stance-record-is-the-delta-between-stated-and-evidenced.md) found the seed sitting structurally empty — dispositions and episodes are elicited by opposite questions, so a founder asked what he thinks returns standing rules and never occasions. The asymmetry is that **half of ADR 0017's evidence is in the room by construction**: a named alternative is a competitor the founder already lives with, and triadic elicitation over three real firms surfaces it without homework. The forfeit is the half that may need a sitting's gap. Stated explicitly because a skill that hands a founder nothing after two hours has a retention problem `to-stance` accepted deliberately and this one does not have to.

## The lifecycle attaches to the layer, not to the file

[#141](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/141) is open and decides the artifact set, and [#137](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/137) has already overturned the two-artifact count in favour of three layers — durable and internal, the revisable communicated position, and a disposable brief. Every rule below therefore attaches to a **layer**, so that nothing here can be invalidated by an artifact set decided in parallel. #137 supplies a discriminator that is checkable rather than editorial, Aaker's audience test: would this line ever be shown to a customer?

**#141 inherits one obligation from this decision: each artifact it lands must declare which layer it is.** Without that label the rules below have nothing to attach to.

## Approval keeps a subject here that it lost in `to-stance`, and it attaches to the claim

ADR 0012 refused both the marker and the gate of the [approval & completion gate pattern](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/9), and its reason was sharper than "nothing downstream gates on this": under ADR 0010 **no agent ever writes the founder-said layer**, so for a position there is no drafted state to leave and the marker would record a transition that cannot occur.

**That reason does not transfer.** Under ADR 0017 the agent classifies claims, applies the bar live, routes failures and assembles a set for a closing read. Drafted-to-approved has a real subject here. **The marker therefore survives and the gate does not** — the gate is refused by the same sibling standing that [ADR 0008](0008-stance-record-replaces-persona-as-the-artifact-category.md) established, under which a gate is the first step toward the artifact becoming an authority.

**But not #9's artifact-level marker.** `approved_by`/`approved_at` in frontmatter works for a one-shot vision; on an artifact that accretes it goes stale the moment the second sitting adds a claim, and re-stamping the file each sitting asserts the founder re-approved material they never revisited. **The stamp attaches to the claim** — the unit ADR 0017's bar already acts on — and the artifact's approval state is **computed**: approved exactly when no claim lacks a stamp.

Two consequences follow for free. A partial artifact reads correctly with no "partial" vocabulary to invent, because an unstamped claim is visibly drafted. And the chain skills' absolute rule — *"a session that ends before approval produces no file… no partial draft, no stub, no empty slug directory"* — **does not transfer**, because it exists to protect a gate this artifact does not have. **Claims are written incrementally as they are admitted and stamped**, so an interrupted two-hour sitting loses nothing. Only admitted-and-stamped claims are written by this rule; whether elicited-but-inadmissible material persists at all is [#147](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/147)'s to decide.

The cost is stated rather than hidden: **the artifact no longer has a single line a human can grep for approval.** That is a genuine ergonomic loss and the strongest argument for keeping #9's shape, and it is accepted. This is a second divergence from #9, reasoned differently from ADR 0012's, and — like that one — explicitly **not** proposed as an amendment to #9, whose four chain skills are not in view here.

## The freeze falls between the evidence and the claim

The map's standing fog offered two horns, [#8](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/8)'s in-place revision and ADR 0011's frozen-plus-dated-append, and #137 offered a third — the durable half freezes, the communicated half does not. **None is adopted as posed, because ADR 0017 moved the unit underneath the question.** A brand claim is admitted by a **named particular**. That particular is historical and dated and cannot become false; the claim it supports is present-tense and absolutely can, because the competitor pivots or the founder takes the segment they once refused. The seam is not durable-versus-communicated at all. It is **evidence versus claim**, and it cuts through every layer.

- **A named particular is append-only, in every layer.** It is ADR 0011's dated episode reached again from an unrelated direction, and nothing edits it; a change in the world is an append.
- **A revisable-layer claim revises in place** per #8, with a dated revision marker. Aaker's wording is the licence — *"the **current** positioning often emphasizes the brand vision elements that are credible and deliverable"* — which makes revisability a design property of that layer rather than a concession.
- **A durable-layer claim is superseded, not edited**, with the old claim left visible and dated at the point it stopped holding. Under #138's fourth failure mode — *aspiration recorded as fact* — a durable claim needing revision is usually evidence the original was inadmissible, and Collins & Porras name the cost of hiding that: *"this will create justifiable cynicism and destroy the power of your core values."* Making the correction visible and expensive is the point, not a side effect.

The contrast with [ADR 0015](0015-two-legal-routing-lines-are-correct-and-a-position-is-never-superseded.md), where a position is never superseded, is deliberate and the two are not in tension: a stance position records conduct that actually happened and cannot stop having happened, while a brand claim asserts something in the present tense that can stop being true. Different objects, opposite rules, recorded together so the pair does not later read as an inconsistency.

**Supersession is triggered mechanically, by a change in the set of particulars.** [ADR 0013](0013-routing-line-extent-is-fixed-revision-is-a-wording-act.md) settled this shape for the stance index — *"its extent is fixed by the record and only its wording is authored"* — and it transposes directly: a claim's extent is fixed by its named particulars, and its wording is authored. If the same particulars still support the claim, an edit is a **wording act**: revise in place with dated provenance, in any layer, with no supersession. If the set changes — one is withdrawn, a forfeit is reversed, a new one is needed to carry it — it is a supersession. Nobody has to judge whether an edit was "substantive," and a lifecycle that made clearer phrasing expensive would simply produce worse phrasing.

## Two start modes, and a re-entry re-checks the world rather than the founder

Three modes were plausible — a first sitting, a continuation days later with homework outstanding, and a re-entry months on. **Two are adopted, and the discriminator is the queue rather than elapsed time.** A sitting with an **outstanding homework queue** is a *continuation* and opens on the homework. A sitting with an **empty queue against an existing artifact** is a *re-entry*. A first sitting is the degenerate re-entry against an artifact that does not exist yet. No mode flag is stored and no duration threshold is tuned; the mode is read off what is already on disk.

**A re-entry opens with a currency pass over the named particulars, never over the claims.** ADR 0012 refused record-level expiry for `to-stance` on the ground that *"the stale-able part is the part that cannot be retrieved"* — episodes are retrospective, and only the quarantined residue goes stale. **Here that inverts exactly**: the claim is present-tense and retrievable, so the stale-able part *is* the retrievable part, and a currency device has a subject here that it lacked there.

The pass asks about the world, not about the claim. Not *"does this still hold?"*, which walks straight into #138's third failure mode — the founder who has pre-decided and wants validation will re-affirm their own copy — but *"is that firm still running the opposite?"* and *"did you end up taking that segment after all?"*, which are checkable. This is Kelly's difference method applied a second time, and ADR 0017's *presence check, not judgement* transposed from admission to currency. Any change routes through the revision rules above.

**An already-admitted claim is never re-tested against the bar.** Only its particulars are re-checked, or every sitting relitigates the last one. What a re-entry finds when the prior sitting admitted nothing is #147's, not this decision's.

## The queue is the come-back device, and progress is read but never scored

`to-stance` stores a next-sitting date (ADR 0010), checked passively on invocation in [ADR 0007](0007-to-milestone-cadence-target-check-in-passive-surfacing.md)'s shape. Here the determinant is not time but material: ZMET's seven-to-ten days of homework is *part of the instrument*, and a date arriving with an empty agenda brings back a founder with nothing new to say. **The homework is stored and the date is derived from it** — Seidman's three-days-to-a-week interval, whose stated reason is that it "allows time for the participant to mull over the preceding interview but not enough time to lose the connection between the two." ADR 0010's ceiling is inherited unchanged and unimproved: a date makes the reminder cheap, it does not make the sitting happen. The derived date is **surfaceable**; *who* checks it is composition and belongs to [#144](https://github.com/98kb/skills/issues/144).

**There is no terminal completion state.** Nothing downstream gates, so "done" has no consumer other than the founder deciding they can act. **A layer is *usable* when its required fields carry admitted claims** — derived per layer, exactly ADR 0012's derived-fitness move, with #141 fixing which fields are required. The disposable layer is generated on demand and is never done in any sense.

**The progress read is never a number.** A denominator is an instruction to fill it, and the only way to fill a brand field on demand is to admit a weak claim — which would re-introduce at the reporting layer the exact mush the bar keeps out at the admission layer. Aaker's mechanism transfers without modification: strategists *"continuously weed out those aspects that they feel are not worth communicating"* the moment they believe there is a target. The read names **what is missing and what would close it**, never a fraction, a percentage or a score.

## An uncosted claim closes two ways, and never on a timer

ADR 0017 created the **uncosted claim** and made it closable, where a `residue` is frozen, but did not say how it closes. It closes **admitted**, when a later sitting supplies the missing forfeit — a real dated act, never a better argument — or **withdrawn**, when the founder drops it; dated either way. It does **not** close by persistence: an uncosted claim that survives three sittings is still uncosted, and no amount of the founder continuing to believe it converts anything. Without that clause the quarantine leaks on a timer, which is the one way a quarantine fails silently.

## What is not recorded, and what that costs

**There is no sitting artifact**, against the precedent of `to-milestone`, which makes each gate-check a durable Milestone. Everything a sitting produces already has a home: an admitted claim carries its stamp, a currency-pass finding is an append to the particular it concerns, unfinished business is a queue entry, and an insisted-on claim is an uncosted entry. The dated series is therefore reconstructible, and a log would be a second store holding a copy of what the first already implies — ADR 0012's stale-or-mutable trap, arriving on a growing record.

One consequence is accepted explicitly: **"this class reached saturation" has nowhere to live**, so a later sitting cannot cheaply distinguish a class that was exhausted from one that was abandoned. If that bites, the fix is a field on the queue, not a sitting artifact.
