## The three gaps, and what they are

**1. A costly act with no decision attached.** *"You take thousand of hotdog
picture yourself — plate, hand, night, close, far. Nobody else have that, because
taking picture of hotdog for one month is boring and they have salary to protect."*

This is the most decision-informative sentence in the file and **nothing in the
component set holds it**. It is not a position — it names no trade-off he is
weighing. It is not a decision episode in McClelland's sense — no counterpart, no
said thing, no felt thing, no moment. It is *revealed* preference in its purest
form: a month of tedium, paid in, with a stated reason. The component set has
slots for what he says he would trade and for a dated incident in which he traded,
and none for **a sustained costly behaviour that demonstrates a disposition
without ever having been a decision**.

Worth #86's attention because the bar was adopted precisely to buy revealed over
expressed preference, and here the revealed evidence is present and inadmissible.

**2. A meta-disposition about which questions are worth answering.**
*"A question about feelings, mission, values… gets a flat, useless answer until it
is re-asked as something concrete."*

Half impersonation-layer (it is written as interview behaviour) and half a real
standing disposition about what counts as a usable question. If it is real it is
load-bearing, because it predicts the *record's own* coverage: an artifact
elicited from this founder will be systematically empty exactly where the
questions were abstract, and the register will not show that as a silence because
nobody logged it as a question opened. **Filed as a gap rather than resolved** —
it may be an artefact of the source, and only a live sitting could tell.

**3. Adoption by re-derivation.** *"Suggestions get 'No' first, and only get
adopted if you re-derive them yourself a moment later and present them as
obviously your own idea."*

Related to R003 but not the same: R003 is refusal on origin, this is
*acceptance* on origin — the same input adopted once relabelled. It matters to a
downstream reader because it says the record's positions will be silently
unstable under attribution, which is Tetlock's rhetorical-reframing finding
arriving from an unexpected direction. No slot for it: it is neither a position
nor an episode, it is a **rule about how positions change**.
