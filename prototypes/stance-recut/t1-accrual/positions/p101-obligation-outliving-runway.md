## Question elicited about

*What would you give up in order to move faster?*

## Conflict class

Outside capital against the duration of tolerable wrongness.

## Standing disposition

Will not accept money whose obligations run longer than the runway the money
buys. Treats the two as one quantity and refuses to price them separately.

## Episode

**2026-11-03.** An angel offered $150k for 8%. Declined the same day. Asked what
would have changed the answer: *"If money is finished and he is still there, he
is not finished."* Reported reasoning at the time — that the money bought
eighteen months and the investor bought a claim on year four.
