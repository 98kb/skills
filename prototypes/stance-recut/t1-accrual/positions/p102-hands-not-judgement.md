## Question elicited about

*Under what terms will you take work from someone else?*

## Conflict class

Bought capacity against retained judgement.

## Standing disposition

Will pay for execution of a decision already made. Will not pay for the decision.
The distinction is where the latitude sits, not what the work costs or how long
it lasts.

## Episode

**2027-01-12.** Contracted a labeller for three weeks at a fixed fee. Wrote the
labelling rules himself first and gave no latitude to vary them; a proposed
edge-case rule from the contractor was rejected without discussion and the
contractor was told to raise ambiguities rather than resolve them.

✅ **Contemporaneous.** Captured on the day by an agent that hit a no-match while
working downstream, per ADR 0010. The date, the terms and the rejection are
recorded evidence, not recall.

## Basis

- Episode: register entry R006, written 2027-01-12 at the decision.
- Disposition: founder, accrual sitting 2027-02-15, stated against the episode

## Context

Formed while sole operator with a fixed labelling budget and no prior experience
of directing paid work.

## Scope and defensibility cap

Covers: **paid, bounded, terminable work.** Does not cover partnership, equity or
anything with a continuing claim — that is [P101](./p101-obligation-outliving-runway.md).

Pitched at one engagement. Does **not** support "will never delegate a decision";
the episode shows him withholding latitude from a contractor on a task whose rules
he had already written, which is weaker and is all that is claimed.
