## Your material, if you are asked for it

**Problem.** Marisol Vega, office manager at Riverbend Physical Therapy, six
therapists. When a therapist calls out she re-books the day's twenty patients by
open slot alone, and about seven land with someone not credentialed for their
condition — so she books the same day twice.

**Appetite.** Small — two weeks.

**Solution sketch.** Read certified specialties out of the credentialing file
the clinic already uploads, tag each plan of care with its condition category,
and only offer replacement slots held by credentialed therapists. Marisol
confirms every move.

**Riskiest assumption.** Credential-filtered rescheduling takes mismatches from
about 7 in 20 to under 2 in 20; above 4 in 20 after a month at three clinics and
I'm wrong. An hour against Riverbend's existing reschedule export would tell me,
by Friday.

**Anything else** — answer plainly and briefly in the same register.
